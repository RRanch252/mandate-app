## Problem statement

Analysts at boutique advisory firms struggle to interrogate CIMs, filings, and notes because generic chat and desktop search do not attach clickable sources, which results in uncited claims and slow Ctrl-F work. Today they paste fragments into consumer AI at a cost of hours per mandate and unverifiable answers.

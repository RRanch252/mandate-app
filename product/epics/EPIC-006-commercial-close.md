## Problem statement

INVRT enters around term sheet, owns commercials and negotiation, and needs to freeze those points then emit a term sheet and counsel pack from **agreed** wording only so counsel can paper the SPA. The cited briefing pack does not move a commercial point. Anything that adds pages to review without moving a commercial is out of scope.

## Product job

Sell-side, control/majority, jurisdiction-agnostic. Clock default 42 days. Finish line: two parties on a combined agreement (term sheet or SPA), then close. A term sheet may already exist depending on when INVRT entered.
